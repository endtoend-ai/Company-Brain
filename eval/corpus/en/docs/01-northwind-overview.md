# Northwind Labs — Company Overview

Northwind Labs is a business-to-business software company headquartered at 525 Market Street,
San Francisco, California. It was founded in 2019 and employs 180 people across San Francisco,
Austin, and a remote engineering team. Northwind builds a supply-chain signal platform that helps
operations teams detect supplier, logistics, and customer-risk events before they become
financial losses.

## Products

- **Signal Graph** — a live map of suppliers, customers, shipments, and contracts.
- **Pulse** — anomaly alerts for delays, price spikes, and compliance exceptions.
- **Compass** — planning and scenario simulation for procurement and customer success.

## Business profile

Northwind Labs reports $48 million in annual recurring revenue (ARR) for fiscal year 2026. The
customer base includes Acme Health, Summit Manufacturing, Pacific Union Bank, and Atlas Industrial.
Northwind is SOC 2 Type II certified, supports HIPAA workloads under a business associate agreement,
and complies with the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).
