# Travel and Expense Policy (v3)

Effective date: January 1, 2026. Owner: Finance. Applies to all Northwind Labs employees and
contractors traveling on company business.

## Lodging

Nightly hotel caps, excluding taxes and resort fees:

| City | Nightly cap |
| --- | --- |
| San Francisco | $280 |
| New York | $320 |
| Austin | $220 |
| Other U.S. cities | $200 |

Travelers may exceed the cap when a conference hotel is required, but must attach the conference
registration and obtain manager approval before booking.

## Meals

- Daily meal allowance: $75 per person.
- Client entertainment above $150 requires pre-approval from a vice president.
- Alcohol is reimbursable only for client entertainment and only with pre-approval.

## Receipts and reimbursement

- Itemized receipts are required for any expense above $75.
- Submit receipts within 30 days of the trip; reimbursements older than 90 days are not paid.
- Use the corporate card for airfare, lodging, and ground transport.
- Personal card expenses are reimbursed only when the corporate card is unavailable.

## Air travel

- Economy class is required for flights under six hours.
- Premium economy is allowed for flights longer than six hours.
- Business class requires CFO approval.

## Approval thresholds

| Total trip cost | Approver |
| --- | --- |
| Under $2,000 | Direct manager |
| $2,000 to $10,000 | Vice president |
| Above $10,000 | Chief Financial Officer |

Questions go to finance@northwindlabs.com.
