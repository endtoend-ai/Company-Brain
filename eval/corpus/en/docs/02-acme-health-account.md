# Acme Health — Account Plan

## Account summary

Acme Health is a hospital network based in Boston, Massachusetts. It operates 12 hospitals and
employs roughly 9,000 people. Acme Health has been a Northwind Labs customer since 2023 and uses
Signal Graph to monitor supplier risk across its clinical supply chain.

- Annual recurring revenue: $1,200,000
- Contract: three-year agreement
- Renewal date: November 30, 2026
- Account executive: Maya Chen
- Customer success manager: Sarah Johnson
- Executive sponsor (customer): Dr. Lena Ortiz, Chief Technology Officer
- Operational sponsor (customer): Grace Kim, Vice President of Operations

## Project Atlas

Project Atlas is the Acme Health deployment of Signal Graph across three hospitals: Boston
Medical Center, Cambridge Children's, and Worcester Regional. The deployment depends on an Epic
integration that streams purchase orders and inventory events into Northwind's ingestion pipeline.

- Technical owner at Northwind: Alex Rivera, Solutions Engineering
- Integration deadline: January 31, 2027
- Current scope: three hospitals in phase one, with an option to extend to all 12 hospitals.

## Risks and mitigation

The primary renewal risk is a delay in the Epic interface certification. Acme Health's integration
team has limited availability in Q4 FY26, and a missed January deadline would push the phase-one
go-live into the next fiscal year.

Mitigation plan:

1. Weekly integration standup led by Alex Rivera and Grace Kim.
2. Freeze the Epic API contract by November 15, 2026.
3. Provide Acme Health with a HIPAA audit export by December 1, 2026.
4. Maya Chen delivers the renewal proposal by November 15, 2026.

## Open opportunities

- Extend Project Atlas to the remaining nine hospitals in fiscal year 2027.
- Add Pulse alerting for cold-chain shipments.
- Add an executive dashboard for Grace Kim's operations team.
