# Summit Manufacturing — Account Plan

## Account summary

Summit Manufacturing is an industrial components manufacturer based in Austin, Texas. It operates
six plants across Texas, Tennessee, and Ohio and has been a Northwind Labs customer since 2024.

- Annual recurring revenue: $450,000
- Renewal date: February 15, 2027
- Account executive: Jordan Lee
- Customer success manager: Sarah Johnson
- Executive sponsor (customer): Robert Hayes, Chief Operating Officer
- Operational sponsor (customer): Ana Silva, Director of Operations

## Project Aurora

Project Aurora is Summit Manufacturing's migration from SAP ECC to SAP S/4HANA. Northwind supplies
the supplier-risk and shipment-delay signals that feed Summit's procurement dashboards. Skyline
Networks is the implementation partner responsible for the SAP data migration and for loading
historical purchase orders into Signal Graph.

- Partner: Skyline Networks
- Partner lead: Emily Carter
- Planned go-live: March 2027
- Current status: data migration is three weeks behind schedule.

## Risks

1. The SAP data migration slipped three weeks because of incomplete vendor master data.
2. Skyline Networks has one open senior consultant role on the project.
3. Summit's procurement team has not finished user acceptance testing for the new dashboards.

## Mitigation plan

- Jordan Lee runs a biweekly partner review with Skyline Networks.
- Ana Silva assigns two additional data stewards to clean vendor master records.
- Northwind provides a staging environment with sample S/4HANA extracts until February 1, 2027.
